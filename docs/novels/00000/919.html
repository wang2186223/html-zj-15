No.1

"You brought this on yourself," Alexander stated coldly. "Take it like a man."

Helpless, Vincent could only endure the beating, shrinking further into himself.

This only infuriated Isabella more. Her rage boiled over. "Damn you! My life was ruined by a worthless piece of trash like you?! You disgusting pig!"

She hauled him up again, ready to hurl him straight through the window.

Vincent panicked. If she threw him out, he'd be crippled for life... if he survived at all!

Was Isabella insane? Did she actually intend to kill him?

"Wait, wait!" he shrieked. "Stop! It wasn't me! I wasn't the one who slept with you that night!"

"Liar!"

No.2

She tightened her grip and launched him through the shattered window.

Crash!

Vincent flew through the broken frame, splintered wood scattering everywhere. The violence of the scene resembled a high-stakes action film.

Alexander stared, stunned. Since when had Isabella become so strong? He remembered a weak, shy girl. This woman was pure power.

Vincent landed hard on the ground outside, blood spurting from his mouth. He groaned weakly, "Not me... really wasn't me...! Why won't you believe me...?!"

Isabella's eyes narrowed. What did he mean, it wasn't him? Her investigation was thorough. Victoria had paid him. The evidence was clear.

She leaped through the ruined window, grabbed his arm, and started dragging him away. "Save your excuses. I'm taking you to the police!"

She might not be pressing charges against Victoria yet, but she needed this evidence secured.

Five years of pain. They would all pay dearly for it.

No.3

Victoria, watching from the shadows, stamped her foot in frantic anxiety. What now? What could she do?!

If Isabella got Vincent to the police station, it was over for her!

Victoria had planned everything so carefully. She wanted Isabella to fall hopelessly for Alexander again. Not only had that failed, but now Isabella was taking Vincent away!

Victoria was about to rush out when Vincent, being dragged raw across the rough ground, burst out in agony, unable to bear the pain any longer. "Boss Montgomery, please! I beg you! It wasn't me! I swear I wasn't the one in that room with you!"

I did follow Victoria's instructions to enter the karaoke room at The Grand Monarch.

But the room was completely empty.

Victoria locked the door from the outside to prevent either of us from escaping.

I assumed the plan had failed and I wouldn't get my payment.

Then the Syndicate Leader actually brought you back to the room!

No.4

The man who slept with you was the Blackthorn Syndicate Leader!

Sebastian Blackwood cried out in genuine agony. His voice carried raw sincerity, completely devoid of deception.

Isabella Montgomery paused. She turned and fixed him with a piercing stare. The Blackthorn Syndicate Leader? You mean Sebastian Blackwood?

Yes! Exactly! Sebastian noticed her hesitation and seized the opportunity. When he brought you back, he handed you over to me. Told me to act like everything went according to plan. Dawn was already breaking. You looked completely disheveled. It was obvious he was the one who slept with you! How could I ever dare touch a woman the Syndicate Leader claimed!

Besides, Victoria and Mr. Whitmore arrived to collect you just minutes later. I never laid a finger on you. Not even a single touch! I swear to God! Ask Mr. Whitmore or Victoria if you don't believe me. My clothes were still perfectly intact. I hadn't even taken off my socks! Sebastian glanced desperately toward Alexander, silently pleading for support.

Isabella didn't bother looking at Alexander. Her mind raced, processing this new information. Alexander had told her she was assaulted at The Grand Monarch, not The Ritz-Carlton!

She had been taken to The Ritz-Carlton to create the illusion she slept with Alexander.

Now it made sense why the hotel staff mentioned seeing two people dragging her inside.

So that was the twisted plan!

No.5

Did that mean Isabella somehow managed to leave the karaoke room before Sebastian entered, encountered Sebastian Blackwood, and was assaulted by him?

Victoria watched from a distance, equally stunned. Was Isabella really this ruthless? Determined to destroy her completely? They had already apologized! Why couldn't she just let it go?!

Alexander watched Isabella's retreating figure, a sinister smirk curling his lips. He had never intended to let her leave easily tonight.

He had to have her. No matter what.

He gave a subtle signal to Sebastian and Victoria before chasing after Isabella.

Victoria breathed a sigh of relief. Thankfully, they had a backup plan. It had to work. No excuses.

She approached Sebastian. You're quite the storyteller! She actually bought it! Come on upstairs. Your reward is waiting.

No.6

O...okay... Sebastian struggled to his feet, supporting his injured body. He moved toward his hut as quickly as his wounds allowed.

Everything he said was the absolute truth. He desperately wanted to clarify that. He genuinely never touched Isabella. But explaining further was pointless. Surviving that terrifying woman was enough for now.

Meanwhile...

Isabella navigated the village paths from memory. Sebastian's words echoed relentlessly in her mind.

She escaped the room before Sebastian arrived. Slept with another man.

That man was Sebastian Blackwood.

She remembered seeing Sebastian before, during her visit to the Blackthorn base. He was hauntingly beautiful, devilishly handsome. Isabella, back then, was considered ugly and terrible-looking. Why would a powerful Syndicate Leader ever sleep with someone like her? Something felt profoundly wrong about this entire story. Had she missed a crucial piece of the puzzle?

Isabella kept walking, her mind swirling with unanswered questions.

The path grew stranger with each step.

No.7

She was certain she had passed this exact spot before.

Why was she circling back to the same location?

She recalled it taking less than thirty minutes to reach Rex’s hut from the village entrance.

Yet she had been walking for over an hour now.

Why hadn’t she found the exit?

She halted abruptly, scanning her surroundings.

Moonlight cast a dim glow over the landscape.

Nothing seemed unusual—just a cluster of huts sitting quietly in the distance.

Maybe she had been too lost in thought to notice her path.
