Chapter 1101

No.1

Victoria paled, her face a mask of sheer horror. He was banishing her? To that distant, unforgiving continent?

“What’s next?” Senior Theodore scoffed. “You believe this is unfair? When Isabella made an error, she endured five years in Africa before returning. Your transgression today is far more severe than hers ever was!”

Back then, Alexander and Victoria had accused Isabella of infidelity, claiming she carried another man’s child. They alleged Alexander discovered the truth and accidentally pushed her down the stairs. Essentially, Isabella’s only fault was alleged unfaithfulness. But Victoria? She conspired with her almost brother-in-law and plotted to steal her sister’s inheritance!

This was utterly unforgivable!

Senior Theodore remembered the video evidence clearly. His voice turned icy. “Marcus Donovan, escort her out.”

Marcus Donovan was Senior Theodore’s most formidable bodyguard, a powerfully built man. He advanced toward Victoria immediately.

Panic seized Victoria. She understood the gravity now. Her reputation was destroyed. The Montgomery family despised her! Being banished to Africa would mean the end.

Desperate, she clutched Eleanor’s arm. “Mother, help me! I’ve learned my lesson! I won’t repeat this! I refuse to go to such a place! I can’t!”

Eleanor’s heart ached watching Victoria’s tears. But she was horrified by Victoria’s actions and true nature. Outwardly, Victoria supported Alexander and Isabella. Secretly, she seduced Alexander and schemed to seize Isabella’s assets!

No.2

She could no longer defend a daughter like this.

Tears welled in Eleanor’s eyes as she pulled her arm away. “Victoria, you must go to Africa. It’s for your own good. For everyone’s sake.”

Victoria trembled violently. Even Eleanor agreed to this exile? Her mistake was huge, but not this severe! Five years away would erase her standing in the Montgomery family. Worse, Isabella would find ways to eliminate her in Africa. Or steal everything she had left, leaving her a discarded orphan!

She gripped Eleanor’s hand tightly, wailing hysterically. “Mother, Father, Grandfather, I’ll apologize! I’ll stay away from Alexander forever! I made a terrible mistake, but don’t send me there! I’ll do anything you ask! Please, I beg you, don’t cast me out…! Waa…!”

Her voice was raw. She transformed into a pitiful, innocent young woman.

Gabriella once said her tears were her greatest weapon. If she could just stay in the city, she could find a way back! With that thought, she collapsed loudly onto the floor. Her limbs went limp as she sobbed wretchedly. “I’m just a fragile girl. I’ve never been apart from you all. I don’t want to leave… I’ll miss you so much…!” She wept again before adding, “I’m still so young. How will I survive there alone…? Waa…!”

Her ragged sobs tugged at many hearts. However furious Eleanor and Richard were, Victoria was still the daughter they raised from childhood. She was their favorite!

Unable to restrain herself, Eleanor turned to Senior Theodore. “Father, perhaps let Victoria stay? We can punish her here as we see fit. She truly is young and foolish…”

“Hah! Did any of you speak for Isabella when she erred? She was merely eighteen then! Was she not young? If Isabella survived Africa alone, can a twenty-three-year-old Victoria truly not manage?” Senior Theodore retorted furiously.

No.3

Oliver nodded in agreement. “Exactly! My father says noble families must uphold strict rules. This sort of person cannot be pardoned!”

Isabella arrived expecting to witness a spectacle, but Nathaniel’s sudden entrance with Christopher disrupted everything. Their bold statements risked exposing their hidden relationship to her family.

She shot a warning glance toward Oliver, but the boy feigned innocence perfectly. “Did I say something wrong?” he pressed, widening his eyes. “Why is no one taking action? Does the Montgomery family condone women like her?”

He paused dramatically, turning toward Nathaniel with feigned urgency. “Daddy, you must never work with these people! They’re defending someone so vile. Families like this can’t be trusted.”

His words sent a chill through the room, particularly unsettling the Whitmores. The Blackwoods' arrival had initially seemed like a promising opportunity, but now it threatened disaster. Would this scandal get them blacklisted?

Margaret Whitmore rushed forward, forcing a smile. “Young Master Blackwood has such a sharp sense of humor! Of course we don’t support her. We fully agree with the decision! Eleanor, Theodore, send Victoria to Africa immediately. She shouldn’t remain in the country any longer.”

Theodore Montgomery understood the gravity of the situation. Victoria’s reputation was irreparably damaged, and offending the Blackwoods was not an option.

He gestured grimly to his head of security. “Marcus, take her home and ensure she packs her belongings.”

“Yes, sir,” Marcus Donovan replied, stepping forward to forcibly remove Victoria.

No.4

Pale with rage, Victoria felt nothing but bitter hatred. This was all Isabella’s doing, yet everyone turned against her. Nathaniel’s interference had only made things worse.

A woman like Isabella didn’t deserve such protection. She didn’t deserve Nathaniel Blackwood!

Her fury finally erupted. “Isabella Montgomery! Everyone will learn the truth about what you did today! Karma will destroy you for framing me! I will never forgive you! Never!”

Her hysterical screams echoed through the hall, dripping with venom.

The guests were stunned. No one had expected the gentle Victoria to reveal such a vicious side. A few even wondered if Isabella might truly be at fault.

But Isabella remained unbothered. The evidence she held was more than enough to ruin Victoria completely. Her priority now was to leave, to put distance between herself and Nathaniel before their relationship was exposed.

She turned to Theodore. “Grandfather, I’ll leave the rest to you. I need to be alone.” Without another word, she walked away, her white gown flowing behind her. Her departure painted a tragic picture, and sympathetic sighs followed her exit.

Theodore glanced at Reginald, signaling him to approach. “Mr. Blackwood, thank you for supporting the Montgomerys today. If you have time, would you care to stay for tea?”

“My apologies,” Nathaniel declined smoothly. “I came to discuss an urgent business matter with Isabella. I must excuse myself.” Taking Christopher’s hand, he followed Isabella out.

No.5

Theodore watched them leave, his expression deeply contemplative.

Alexander’s vision turned crimson in that moment.

He was Isabella’s man.

Why was an outsider like Nathaniel pursuing her?

He rose to his feet, ready to chase after her.

Theodore Montgomery’s voice cut through the air like ice. “Stay where you are, Alexander! I forbid you from ever seeing my granddaughter again. If you disobey, I will never forgive you!”

Margaret Whitmore interjected sharply. “Theodore, what are you saying? We’ve already disciplined Alexander for his mistakes. He’s been beaten and scolded. Isn’t that sufficient?”

Theodore’s gaze was frigid. “If I demanded a Montgomery man sleep with two of your granddaughters, would you accept that? Do not take advantage of us and then cry for fairness!

“Our business partnership may continue, but my granddaughters will have no further contact with the Whitmore family!” His words were final and unyielding.

No.6

He turned and strode away without looking back.

Richard Montgomery and Eleanor Kensington followed closely behind him.

They had initially hoped to contain the scandal’s damage.

They wanted Isabella to proceed with the ceremony and marry Alexander.

But after Nathaniel Blackwood’s intervention, their fears vanished.

Alexander was the source of all this chaos.

He was first involved with Victoria.

Then he changed his mind and pursued Isabella.

When the Montgomery family finally agreed, he was caught in bed with Victoria on his wedding day!

No.7

He destroyed the reputations of two Montgomery women in one blow.

If not for the business ties between the families, William Kensington would have beaten Alexander senseless.

With the Montgomery family gone, the Whitmores were left to handle the messy aftermath.

“Damn it!” Margaret hissed under her breath. “What’s their problem? They’re just two women with ruined reputations anyway! Who cares!”

“Shut up!” Alexander snapped at her.

Victor Whitmore kicked his son hard. “You disgraceful boy! Now you’re talking back to your mother? If it weren’t for your actions today, we would be wealthy beyond measure! We wouldn’t be stuck here cleaning up this mess! I should have let your older brothers take over the business. I never should have entrusted it to you!”

Alexander’s face darkened at his father’s words.

He had always been the least favored son in the family.

His parents had wanted a daughter after two sons.
