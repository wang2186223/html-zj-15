NovelVibe

Chapter 577

No.1

“A leading supermodel needs a support staff of at least twenty people. Handling it alone would be exhausting. Sign with SilverPeak Entertainment, and we handle everything for you.”

Isabella was well aware of these requirements. Her real question was about Seraphina Delacroix’s motive. She remembered clearly; Seraphina wanted to sign her to suppress her rising fame. Why would a woman known for her bitterness suddenly offer kindness?

“My apologies,” Isabella stated flatly, her expression unreadable. “But my team is already handling those matters.” She turned and walked toward the VIP exit.

Genevieve moved to follow, but the roped-off VIP aisle stopped her. Only those with exclusive access could proceed. Frustration boiled over. “Isabella Montgomery! A smart person recognizes a good opportunity! I’ve pursued you because I see your value! Don’t throw it away out of pride!”

Isabella didn’t bother to look back. She had no interest in a delusional woman’s rant. As she neared her table, a portly man stepped into her path.

His eyes lit up with recognition. “Isabella Montgomery! What a fortunate encounter! I’ve been trying to get in touch.” He eagerly extended a business card. “A pleasure to meet you.”

Isabella’s gaze dropped to the card without taking it. The embossed text read: Reginald Worthington, Chief Executive Officer, Golden Phoenix Entertainment.

Another entertainment executive.

She raised a cool eyebrow. “What is this about?”

“It’s simple,” he beamed, undeterred by her lack of enthusiasm. “You’ve recently entered the industry. We want to sign you. This meeting feels like fate! You’re a diamond magnate, correct? I made my initial fortune in mining, too. Coal, in my case.”

No.2

He puffed out his chest with pride. “My current net worth sits around fifty billion. I founded Golden Phoenix Entertainment. We’re a major player here. Recognise Xander… what’s-his-name? Or Nevil, the chart-topping singer? They’re both with us!”

The company name sounded familiar. Julian Hawthorne had mentioned them. SilverPeak was number one, Golden Phoenix was a close second. SilverPeak excelled at crafting celebrity images. Golden Phoenix was known for buying fame through massive financial investment. Naturally, they expected a high return. They were notorious for arranging private engagements for their stars. Whispers suggested some actresses earned eight figures in a single night. The darkest rumor was that Reginald started the company primarily to meet women…

Reginald mistook her silence for interest. He smiled, placing a familiar hand on her shoulder. “Isabella, sign with us. You’ll get our absolute best resources. First pick of anything you want. We can negotiate the profit split. Just name your terms!”

Isabella’s eyes flicked coldly to the hand on her shoulder. Her voice remained calm and detached. “Your offer is… persuasive, Mr. Worthington. But I have a dilemma. I just encountered Genevieve Beaumont, SilverPeak’s PR director. She made a very similar proposal. It puts me in a difficult position.”

“What? Genevieve is here? Where?” Reginald’s face darkened instantly. Golden Phoenix and SilverPeak were bitter rivals, constantly fighting over talent and awards. SilverPeak had snatched desired artists from him before. Was Isabella next?

Isabella gestured casually toward the restroom area. “Over there. She was quite vocal. Claimed her company was the industry’s undisputed best. Said Golden Phoenix wasn’t even in the same league. Those were her words.”

“The audacity! Wait right here. I need to have a word with her.” Reginald stormed off in the direction she indicated.

Genevieve was still by the restrooms, typing a furious message to Seraphina Delacroix. A booming voice interrupted her. “Genevieve Beaumont! What is your problem?!”

Genevieve spun around at the sound of her name. Reginald Worthington marched toward her with a scowl. Her lips curled into a mocking smile. "Mr. Worthington, for a CEO, you have zero class. Did your money just buy you arrogance?"

"What's it to you? You think you're better? Ha! A nobody like you shouldn't even dare to stand in my way. Listen carefully—Isabella Montgomery is signing with me. Try to interfere, and you'll regret it," Reginald snarled, hoping to intimidate her.

No.3

She raised a brow. "Isabella? She isn't signing with my agency either."

"Stop lying! You've been competing with SilverPeak from the start. Maybe you need a reality check. Should I expose how your so-called talents are all fakes?" he threatened.

To his surprise, Genevieve laughed. "Go ahead! Let's play this game. You reveal my fakes, and I'll reveal your dirty secrets. Hmph! You earn your money through disgraceful means."

"You... You witch! How dare you? I'll slap that smirk off your face!" Reginald raised his hand, trying to scare her.

Suddenly, something sharp hit the back of his knee. He stumbled forward in pain.

Smack!

His palm accidentally struck Genevieve's cheek.

Genevieve had never been hit since her rise to fame. Even her superiors tread carefully around her. She never expected Reginald would go this far.

Instantly, she saw red. "You arrogant bastard! How dare you hit me? This isn't over!"

Without a second thought, she swung her hand with all her might. A stinging pain shot through her palm as Reginald's head snapped to the side.

No.4

His entire body wobbled from the impact. A bright red palm print appeared on his face, with faint scratch marks from her nails.

Enraged, Reginald lunged at her. All hell broke loose.

His punches were met with her scratches. His kicks were countered by her heels. Pure chaos erupted.

On the sidelines, Isabella watched the scene unfold with satisfaction. A faint smile touched her lips as she turned and walked away.

They deserved this for scheming against her.

Reginald and Genevieve fought like wild animals. Only when hotel staff rushed in did they finally stop.

Even the police had to intervene to separate them.

"What? Isabella refused to sign with you?" Reginald asked, shocked.

"Yes! I told you already. I approached her multiple times, and she rejected me every time. If she wanted to join SilverPeak, she would have done it long ago. You never stood a chance," Genevieve snapped back.

Confused, Reginald muttered, "Then why did she tell me—"

No.5

Their eyes widened in unison as realization struck. They had been played.

"That witch set us up!" Reginald growled. "She pitted us against each other! Damn it... I'll make her pay!"

"Killing her is too easy," Genevieve replied quickly. "Let's join forces and destroy her career instead!" Reginald's eyes lit up. He liked the idea.

Both SilverPeak and Fortune Entertainment were industry giants. They controlled the entertainment world.

If they worked together, crushing a newcomer like Isabella would be simple.

She would suffer for messing with them.

The moment Isabella stepped into the private room, the uproar from Hubert and Genevieve's confrontation flooded the space.

Oliver, mid-bite into a crispy chicken wing, flinched at the sudden noise. "Mommy, what's all that yelling outside?"

"Just some stray dogs fighting." She shut the door firmly, sealing off the chaos.

Isabella settled beside him, gently dabbing his lips with a napkin. "Eat up. You need the energy."

No.6

"Okay! Try this cake, Mommy. It's super good!"

He speared a generous slice with his fork and held it out. Isabella leaned in, letting him carefully feed her.

Warmth spread through her chest. She never realized joy could be found in such simple moments.

Nathaniel watched their interaction from across the table, a slow smile gracing his features.

The meal was perfect.

Twenty minutes later, Nathaniel finished eating. Oliver was still happily munching on fried chicken while Isabella chatted with him. Nathaniel checked his phone for urgent messages.

Dominic's text glared back at him.

[Boss! Emergency! SilverPeak and Fortune Entertainment are colluding to blacklist Isabella! No one will sign her or collaborate. Anyone who does faces industry-wide boycott!]

SilverPeak and Fortune Entertainment controlled the industry's top talent. Defying them meant being relegated to third-tier artists permanently. No company could risk that.

Within an hour, news of Isabella's blackout spread rapidly. Everyone chose the safe side. Paparazzi scrubbed all online traces of her.

No.7

After last night's events, Isabella should have been the hottest new star. Her name dominated every headline and trended across all platforms. Then the blacklist announcement hit, and she vanished from the internet entirely.

Her name was purged from search results and trending topics. It was as if she never existed.

Nathaniel's eyes narrowed dangerously. 'They dare target my woman?'

He kept the news from Isabella, quickly texting Dominic.
