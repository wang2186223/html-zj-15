No.1

“Fine.” Vivienne reluctantly gave in.

Archibald checked his watch. “Three more minutes. Important people are always punctual. I’m going to the restroom. Wait here for her and make good conversation.”

“Okay.” Vivienne agreed, though she wasn’t happy about it.

Her grandfather worried too much about appearances. Vivienne was too preoccupied with the whole Nathaniel and Isabella situation to bother with trivial matters. Still… if this could help her reputation, it might be worth it.

After all, Archibald Worthington valued his reputation immensely and expected his heir to be free from scandals and criticism to avoid future complications.

Vivienne took out a compact mirror to touch up her makeup.

Just then, the sharp click of high heels echoed through the room. Vivienne looked up and saw a woman standing in the doorway.

It was Isabella Montgomery!

Isabella wore a crisp white suit, looking every bit the professional. She spotted Vivienne and glanced at the room number. “Where’s Archibald Worthington?” she asked.

No.2

Vivienne recovered from her surprise and sneered. “How did you find out about this, Isabella? How do you know my grandfather is here? Do you think just anyone can meet him?”

Isabella’s eyes narrowed. So Archibald Worthington was Vivienne’s grandfather?

Since Beatrice Worthington had arranged the meeting, Isabella hadn’t bothered to investigate further, assuming it was just another industry contact. She never expected Archibald to be so invested in cleaning up Vivienne’s mess.

“Even if you get on your knees and beg him, he won’t sell to you. Now get out!”

Her words dripped with venom and disgust. The mere thought of Isabella being with Nathaniel made Vivienne’s stomach turn.

Isabella didn’t even bother rolling her eyes. She simply turned and walked away, her steps steady and confident, radiating an undeniable aura of power.

Vivienne scoffed. A cheap nouveau riche trying to steal Nathaniel from her? She would make her pay dearly.

When Archibald returned to the room, he checked his watch with a puzzled expression. “What’s going on? Didn’t she say she’d be on time? Where is she?”

“Grandpa, why don’t you call and ask? Who would dare keep you waiting?” Vivienne suggested.

No.3

Archibald agreed and took out his phone to call Beatrice. Little did he know…

“Isabella? Where is she?” Nathaniel’s voice was sharp through the phone.

“Mr. Blackwood! She did arrive, but she claimed your granddaughter offended her and forced her departure!” Gloria’s tone was strained with helplessness.

Archibald Worthington’s brow furrowed deeply. The expert he’d pleaded with came, and his own granddaughter drove her away?

He ended the call abruptly, his palm slamming onto the polished mahogany desk. “Vivienne! What is the meaning of this? Do you comprehend how difficult it was to secure this meeting? How could you be so foolish? Do you even care about salvaging your reputation? I, Archibald Worthington, will disinherit you!”

His face flushed a deep crimson, his neatly trimmed mustache seeming to bristle with his rage.

Vivienne had never witnessed her grandfather so enraged. She quickly tried to explain. “Grandfather, I did not dismiss the person you invited! I only removed Isabella Montgomery from the premises!”

She stopped mid-sentence, her eyes narrowing as a dreadful suspicion took hold. “Grandfather… the expert you invited… it wasn’t her, was it?”

“How would I know her name? All I know is she masterminded the Celestial Gala! She transformed Arabella Fontaine and Genevieve Beaumont overnight! One was a perpetual third wheel, the other a faded star! Yet she made them both shine! Your situation would be trivial for her! Why must you always let your temper destroy everything?!”

No.4

Archibald’s roar echoed in the lavish study. It took every ounce of his self-control not to strike his granddaughter.

The truth crashed down on Vivienne. Who else could have engineered those turnarounds but Isabella Montgomery?

The so-called expert her grandfather revered was that woman all along!

She had dressed meticulously, prepared to humble herself before the expert… only to find it was Isabella? Her grandfather wanted her to beg Isabella for help?

Fury ignited within her. “Grandfather, you are only seeing the surface! You have no idea how vile that woman truly is! She’s sleeping with Nathaniel Blackwood!”

“What?!” Archibald’s white eyebrows shot up in stunned disbelief.

Vivienne met his gaze with fierce intensity. “The expert you begged to come is Isabella Montgomery! Recently returned from abroad! The disgraced adopted daughter who brought shame to the Montgomery family!”

“Grandfather, do you truly believe she possesses such talent? She has Nathaniel Blackwood backing her! For all we know, he is the one providing her with all those strategies!”

“Vivienne, you know I despise lies. Where is your proof?” Archibald’s expression was stern. “Didn’t Reginald Blackwood himself confirm your engagement to Nathaniel by month’s end?”

No.5

Vivienne let out a bitter, scornful laugh. “That was his parents’ arrangement! Nathaniel wants nothing to do with me! Isabella has him completely ensnared! I witnessed it myself! They were all over each other! They even live under the same roof!”

Seeing the raw honesty in her eyes, Archibald’s initial rage began to cool, replaced by dawning horror. “Is this true? Nathaniel rejects you… for an adopted daughter from the entertainment industry? An actress?”

“Grandfather, have I ever lied to you?” Vivienne guided him to his chair, her movements suddenly gentle as she poured him tea. “When Seraphina first told me, I refused to believe it. I thought a man of Nathaniel’s stature would never be interested in someone like her. But I saw it with my own eyes at the Blackwood Estate. Isabella even convinced Oliver to threaten me with pythons to drive me away.”

She relayed the entire humiliating encounter. Archibald’s fist crashed onto the desk again, the sound thunderous in the quiet room.

“Preposterous! This is utterly preposterous! A woman of her dubious background… who knows what filth she’s been involved with! How could Nathaniel possibly be interested in her?”

To Archibald, a man like Nathaniel should only be with someone like his granddaughter Vivienne.

How could an adopted daughter straight from Africa steal him away?

He looked at Vivienne. "Why didn't you tell me sooner? You're my granddaughter! No one gets to bully you. Don't worry, I'll fix this."

"It's fine, Grandpa. We shouldn't act now. It would make me look desperate to marry him," Vivienne commented, her eyes dark with hatred.

No.6

Archibald nodded. "Right. His parents would never accept a daughter-in-law like that woman. But you must watch them closely. Tell me if you need anything."

"Okay." Vivienne raised her coffee cup and took a delicate sip.

To the outside world, she remained the refined and elegant Houston heiress. But no one knew the venomous thoughts swirling in her mind. If they couldn't get photos of Isabella with Nathaniel, then she would just have to... Ugh!

Meanwhile...

Alexander and Victoria.

As always, Alexander was dressed in a sharp suit, radiating the aura of a successful CEO. Victoria wore a soft, pale green maxi dress. Her hair fell over her shoulders, making her look fragile, like a gentle breeze could carry her away.

Alexander frowned. He instinctively dropped Victoria's hand the moment he saw Isabella.

"Isabella, what are you doing here?"

No.7

Since Isabella had rejected his confession, Victoria offered to help him win her back. That's why they met at the café. He never imagined they'd run into Isabella of all people...

Victoria noticed Alexander's reaction. A flash of pure hatred flickered in her clear eyes. She quickly masked it, stepping closer to Isabella. "Isabella, I finally found you. You haven't returned to the Montgomery manor, and you're no longer at Rosewood. I've been wanting to apologize. I just never got the chance."

"It was all my fault. I never meant to ruin your wedding. Alexander and I are truly over. What happened that day... it was just closure..."

Victoria lowered her head, feigning pain. A faint blush even colored her cheeks. Underneath her meek words, her real message was clear: So what if Alexander wanted Isabella? He still couldn't resist her body.

Isabella scoffed, adjusting her black sunglasses. "Well, look who it is. Dressed like a common streetwalker. The fake Ms. Montgomery, I presume?"

Her tone was slow and dripping with mockery.

Victoria's face flushed. Did she just call her a prostitute?

She even said 'fake'!
